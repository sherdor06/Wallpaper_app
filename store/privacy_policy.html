Privacy Policy — Wallpapers 4K

Effective date: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wallpapers 4K mobile application ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it on Android or iOS.

1. Information we collect

We do not require accounts and do not collect personal information ourselves.

  • Favorites are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • Downloaded wallpapers are saved to your device's gallery / photo library at your request.
  • The App downloads wallpaper images and a content catalog from our content delivery network (Cloudflare). Standard technical data (such as your IP address) is processed by the CDN to deliver these files, as with any internet download.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use data to show ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interaction data (e.g., ad views and clicks)

This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. Permissions the App uses

  • Internet — to download the wallpaper catalog, images, and ads.
  • Set wallpaper (Android) — to apply a wallpaper you choose.
  • Add to photo library (iOS) — only to save wallpapers you choose to your Photos.

The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

4. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google's policies.

5. Data retention

We do not operate user accounts or store personal data on our servers. Locally stored data (favorites, cached images) can be removed at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
